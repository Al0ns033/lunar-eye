Your first walkthrough
  1. Choose a site in Lunar Explorer or use a saved site in Planning.
  2. Set the arrival date, duration and solar panel configuration.
  3. Select Calculate geometry: calculations run only when you request them.
  4. Read the Sun, Earth and power indicators; open the details for sources and limitations.
  5. Use Play in the top bar or move the timeline to explore the calculated period.

Data and limitations

NASA/JPL Horizons ephemerides · NASA LRO LOLA lunar terrain.

An exploration tool: estimates depend on terrain resolution. Earth visibility indicates a geometric line of sight and does not guarantee a radio link.
